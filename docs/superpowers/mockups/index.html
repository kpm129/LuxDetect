Notes for this review

  • Only the theme checkbox, section links, mode-selection styling, reference-source dropdown and Debug disclosure respond. All measurement controls are visual examples. There is no JavaScript, camera access, storage, calculation or export logic.
  • Latest fit thresholds are reflected: Good at R² ≥ 0.9; Fair at 0.8–0.9; Failed below 0.8. Every other validity check must also pass. Entirely rough references cap the badge at Fair.
  • Light labels shown in the reading examples are illustrative. Proposed v1 behavior: do not assign lux-based sky labels to an uncalibrated brightness index. Editable label boundaries: Dark <50 lx; Overcast 50–<5,000; Shade 5,000–<15,000; Daylight 15,000–50,000 inclusive; Bright sky >50,000. Coverage uses these same bins. Out-of-range readings hide the category.
  • The curve samples, fit statistics and capture progress are synthetic visual examples, not a calibration result. Real iPhone exposure behavior may prevent calibration.
  • Coverage is shown as discrete reference-range bins, not as a claim that every point between the darkest and brightest sample was tested. Weather descriptions are suggestions, not fixed lux values.
  • Diffuser and no-diffuser modes keep their own samples and calibration. Changing the diffuser requires a new calibration. A failed attempt never overwrites a valid calibration. The application will use localStorage; these mockups do not write to storage.
